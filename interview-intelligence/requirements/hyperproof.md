## 5. Stage-by-stage evaluation targets

- **Application (official):** Answer the "4+ years React + Java (Spring Boot)" question honestly. You likely answer "No" with 3 years and Node. Use the essays to show strength:
  - In the testing essay, describe unit, integration and end-to-end layers, test data for edge cases, migrations tested against PostgreSQL, idempotency, and how you prevent data-integrity bugs. They say integrity "is not optional".
  - In the async essay, give a concrete written-update example.
  - If you have any SOC 2 or ISO 27001 exposure (even as an end user of compliance evidence), say so.
- **Take-home (official/corroborated):** Practise timed TypeScript coding problems at easy-to-medium level in a Coderbyte-style environment. Also practise a small full-stack feature with tests. One report mentions "write a unit test for a simple function … why the test was named what it was, how it could be refactored". Testing craft is likely examined.
- **Talent partner chat (official):** 30 minutes. Prepare a crisp story on why GRC and compliance, remote async habits, your employment-model preference, and your salary expectation inside ₹21.4–30 lakh.
- **Three 60-minute 1:1s (official):** Expect a technical deep-dive, a design or architecture discussion, and a hiring-manager conversation. Practise designing a risk register: entities, risk scoring (likelihood × impact for inherent risk, then reduced by control effectiveness for residual risk), control-test scheduling, audit trails and RBAC. Also explain trade-offs aloud, which is a known weak area. One 2025 report says the questions were "HackerRank low-medium".
